# Alternating exterior roots of the two fixed cofactors

Interlacing means that roots of the two polynomials alternate. The result below is independent of which of the two possible physical cofactor orders occurs.

## Hypotheses and original-source notation

Use the family and determinant convention from [the real exterior-pair lemma](f3-real-exterior-pairs.md): 0<e<a<c,0<f<b,d>0 and0<e+d<a−b<c−f. Let A=a−e,B=b+d,r=c−a,q=b−f. Then A>B>0. In odds t=x/(1−x), signed original cofactor numerators are E_i, and L1=a+bt,L2=e−dt,L3=c+ft. Their exact null relations are ΣE_i=ΣL_iE_i=0.

Assume the nonphysical pair of E3 is nonreal.  Since E3(0)>0 and it has no nonpositive real root, E3(t)>0 on t≤0.

## Fixed negative pairs

The source gauge formula for E0 has positive leading coefficient, E0(−∞)<0,E0(−A/B)>0,E0(−1)<0,E0(0)<0. Thus its two negative roots α0<β0 are simple and

α0<−A/B<β0<−1.

The E2 cubic has negative leading coefficient, E2(−∞)>0,E2(−a/b)<0,E2(−1)>0,E2(0)>0. Thus its two negative roots α2<β2 are simple and

α2<−a/b<β2<−1.

Each cubic has exactly one remaining positive root; no discriminant calculation is needed.

## Exact interlacing

At any E0 root, the null relations give

E2=E3*(L3−L1)/(L1−L2).

On t≤0, L3−L1=r−qt>0, and L1−L2=A+Bt. Therefore E2(α0)<0 and E2(β0)>0. The E2 sign is negative exactly between α2 andβ2 on the negative axis. Hence

**α2<α0<β2<β0<−1.**
