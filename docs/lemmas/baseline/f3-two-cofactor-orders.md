# Only two orders of the four physical cofactor roots are possible

Use the F3 family and determinant signs in [the real exterior-pair lemma](f3-real-exterior-pairs.md). Suppose every signed cofactor has one simple physical root $`r_i`$, and no two such roots coincide. The label string $`0231`$ means $`r_0\lt r_2\lt r_3\lt r_1`$; $`1320`$ means $`r_1\lt r_3\lt r_2\lt r_0`$. These are root orders, distinct from the exponent-sum orders.

Here $`A_{\rm gap}=a-e`$, $`B_{\rm gap}=b+d`$, $`r_{\rm gap}=c-a`$, and $`q=b-f`$. A mode is the zero of the beta factor's logarithmic derivative. The mode of $`m_2`$ is $`x=e/(e+d)`$, or $`t=e/d`$ in the positive coordinate $`t=x/(1-x)`$.

## Lemma 1: variable cofactors have no physical root before the m2 mode

In odds t=x/(1−x), the positive rescaling of e3 is

(a+bt)(e−dt)[a−e+(b+d)t]+(ad+be)t(1+t).

Every factor in the first product is positive when0<t≤e/d, and the added term is positive. The analogous expression with(c,f) proves the same for−e1. Hence all physical roots of e1/e3 lie strictly after the m2 mode.

## Lemma 3: two cofactor orders follow directly, without listing every ordering

e2(τ)=−(bc−af)*x(1−x)<0,

so r2<τ. The cofactor relation at τ implies e0(τ)>0, hence r0<τ too.

At r3, λ2<0 by Lemma1 and

e1=−λ2*e2/λ1, e0=−(1−λ2/λ1)e2.

If r3<r1, this gives r0,r2<r3<r1. If r1<r3, it gives r1<r3<r0,r2. At r2,

e0=(λ3−λ1)*e3/λ1,

and the prefactor is positive because r2<τ. Thus the first case has r0<r2 and the second r2<r0. The only orders are0231 and1320.

For word1320, τ is after every cofactor root; for word0231, τ is after r0,r2. This direct proof establishes the two all-one root orders without relying on event enumeration.
