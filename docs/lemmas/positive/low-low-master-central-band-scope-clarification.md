# Why any central actual contact supplies the exterior lemma's source band

Read [the appendix terminology](../../appendix.md#terminology) before this technical lemma. Its variables are local to this document.

At a central actual curvature contact c, H(c)<0 and u0(c)=uC(c)>0.
Therefore Y(c)=H(c)u0(c)<0. Strict low walls have Y(eta)>0 and Y(xi)>0,
so continuity gives a sign-changing root c1 in (eta,c) and another c2 in
(c,xi). Each has odd multiplicity. Since Y is cubic, both are simple:
if either had multiplicity at least three, the other would exceed the
available degree. Its remaining real root is negative because its constant
and leading coefficients are positive, and the product of all three roots
is negative. Thus

Y(r)=R3(r+l)(r-c1)(r-c2), l>0, eta<c1<c2<xi.

There can be no further positive Y roots. This is precisely the interlaced
source-root hypothesis of the whole-exterior-two lemma, and it implies
Y>0 on both whole exterior wall intervals. No canonical physical source
bound loop or nongeneric edge classification is needed.
