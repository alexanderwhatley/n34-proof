# Two contacts on each whole low-wall exterior when a central source band exists

Read [the appendix terminology](../../appendix.md#terminology) before this technical lemma. Its variables are local to this document.

Let P,Q>1, let eta<xi be the roots of H, and let R be a positive-coefficient
cubic. Put Y=R-r(1+r), u0=Y/H. Suppose Y has two simple roots
eta<c1<c2<xi and its third root is -l<0. At any one actual finite common
entropy, each whole exterior interval has at most TWO weighted
normal-curvature contacts. No physical source bound-loop or source-edge
regularity is assumed; comparable geometric outer supports are allowed.

On the left exterior Y>0, H>0, and

u0=d(r+l)(c1-r)(c2-r)/[(eta-r)(xi-r)], d>0.

Its source elasticity eta0=r u0'/u0 has the identity

eta0=r/(r+l)+p-q,
p=r(c1-eta)/[(eta-r)(c1-r)]>0,
q=r(xi-c2)/[(c2-r)(xi-r)]>0.

If eta0>1, then p>q because r/(r+l)<1. Moreover

p'/p=1/r+1/(eta-r)+1/(c1-r)
 >1/r+1/(c2-r)+1/(xi-r)=q'/q>0.

Thus p'>q' and eta0'=l/(r+l)^2+p'-q'>0 at every point with eta0>1.
These universal identities and inequalities are already verified in the
canonical exterior module; their mathematical scope is the whole
interlaced wall interval.

For the one actual curve, its elasticity etaC=r uC'/uC is strictly
decreasing. Also etaC>1 throughout 0<r<eta, since uC/r increases until
alpha=(Q-1)/P>eta. Consider, in x=log r,

F(x)=log(uC(r)/u0(r)).

At each critical point F_x=etaC-eta0=0, so eta0=etaC>1. The implication
above and the strict actual elasticity sign give

F_xx=r(etaC'-eta0')<0.

Every critical point is therefore a nondegenerate strict maximum. The
function tends to minus infinity at both ends of the left interval:
uC tends to zero while u0(0)>0, and u0 tends to plus infinity at eta
while uC remains finite. Hence an interior maximum exists, and there cannot
be two maxima because an intervening minimum would be a critical point.
Thus F has one nondegenerate maximum, is strictly increasing before it and
strictly decreasing after it, and has at most two zero weights. The positive
logarithmic multiplier preserves the orders of uC-u0 contacts.

Reflection r=1/s, P,Q exchanged, and u tilde=u/r preserves all contact
orders and gives the same result on xi<r<infinity. This proves the whole
exterior ceiling without ever restricting to physical source components.
